# Hotel ZGY convenience store

All details in this document are fictional demo facts for Hotel ZGY.

## Store location and hours

The Hotel ZGY convenience store is on the first floor of the hotel. It is open
from 7:00 a.m. to 10:00 p.m.

## Store stock

The Hotel ZGY convenience store stocks drinks, snacks, over-the-counter pain
and cold medication, toothpaste, mouthwash, and souvenirs. For information on
specific brands and sizes, guests need to contact the front desk or visit the
store. Specific brands and sizes are not provided in the hotel information.
