# Hotel ZGY guest services

All details in this document are fictional demo facts for Hotel ZGY.

## Room service

Hotel ZGY room service is available from 6:00 a.m. to 10:00 a.m. and from
7:00 p.m. to 1:00 a.m. Eastern Time. The evening period runs past midnight and
ends at 1:00 a.m. the following morning. The concierge can provide this
information but cannot place an order.

## Luggage storage

After checkout, guests may leave tagged luggage with the front desk until
8:00 p.m. the same day. Overnight luggage storage is not available. The
concierge can explain the policy but cannot arrange storage.

## Hotel shuttle

A hotel shuttle is available at Hotel ZGY. No shuttle schedule or pickup
location is provided in the hotel information; guests should ask hotel staff
for those details.
